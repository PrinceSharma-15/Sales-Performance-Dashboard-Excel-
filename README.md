# Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Excel-

## About the Project

I created this interactive Store Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance and customer behavior. The goal of this project was to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights that can help businesses understand their performance and make better decisions.
The dashboard allows users to filter data by month, product category, and sales channel, making it easy to explore different parts of the dataset.

# What You Can Explore
This dashboard answers questions like:
- How do sales and orders change throughout the year?
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- Do men or women contribute more to total sales?
- Which age group places the most orders?
- What is the status of customer orders?
- Which sales channels bring the most orders?
- # Dashboard Features
- Interactive slicers for Month, Category, and Channel
- Monthly Orders vs Sales comparison
- Sales distribution by Gender
- Top 5 States by Sales
- Order Status analysis
- Age Group vs Gender comparison
- Orders by Sales Channel

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel

# Key Insights
After analyzing the data, I found a few interesting insights:
- Women contribute more to total sales than men.
- Maharashtra is the highest-performing state in terms of sales.
- Most customer orders are successfully delivered.
- Amazon and Myntra generate a large share of total orders.
- Sales and order volumes vary throughout the year, highlighting seasonal trends.
